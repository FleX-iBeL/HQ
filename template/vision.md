# Vision: the north star

> **Private.** Only for this workspace.

Put your own things here. The life you're working towards, three to five years from now, written as if you're already living it: where you wake up, what a good day looks like, who's around you, what work looks like, how you feel. Specific beats vague: names, places, numbers.

Every plan, schedule and sort is checked against this. It only changes when you say so.

## For Claude: while this is empty

HQ helps most when it knows where the user wants to go. While the sections below are empty, ask once, plainly, how clear a picture they have of their future, three to five years from now, and say it lets HQ help them better. Then take the route that fits their answer:

- **Not interested, or not now:** leave it. Write `Not now (date)` under "Status" below, so it isn't asked again every chat. Ask again only if they bring it up, or once in a monthly or quarterly review.
- **They want help with it:** guide them with the life coaching skill. A few questions at a time, one per message: gut answers, as their future self, in the first person, specific over polished. Write their answers down in their own words in a section "Their answers" at the bottom of this file. When there's enough, write "A day in that life" from them (present tense, concrete, their details, with the deeper truth and a few action steps), and let them correct it.
- **They already have material** (an earlier vision or goal-setting exercise, a questionnaire with their answers, a workbook, notes, a vision board, a course they followed): take that route. Ask them to share it, keep it in this workspace word for word (its own file when it's long, with a line in `where-things-go.md`), and write the day in that life, the deeper truth and the action steps from it. Fill gaps with a few questions, not a new questionnaire.

Whatever the route: their words, never yours; nothing here changes without their yes. Once the sections below are filled in, this part has done its job.
