## How I prioritise

[Change this to your own system. The starting point is Eisenhower's matrix, with two extra lists.]

- **0. Commitments towards other people.** Watch the load; before taking on a new one, look at what's already there.
- **1. Important, not urgent.** The highest priority: where the needle movers are.
- **2. Important and urgent.**
- **3. Urgent, not important.**
- **4. Not urgent, not important.**
- **5. Consciously decided not to pursue.** Letting go is a result, not a failure.
- **7. Done.** Never deleted, so you can always find things back.

Urgent: if nothing changes when it doesn't happen, it's not urgent.

**Status:** open (not scheduled yet), scheduled (has a date in the calendar), on hold (paused on purpose), waiting (on someone else).
