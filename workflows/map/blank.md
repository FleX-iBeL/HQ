# The map

> **Private.** Only for this workspace.

How the life you want becomes this week. An upside-down pyramid: the far future on top, wide; each layer below narrower and closer, until it's today. Each layer is checked against the one above it, and looking back feeds every layer again.

Put your own things here: you fill it in together with your agent in a few life coaching sessions on a call, one layer at a time, in your words. Start the call with something like "let's go through my map". The guide your agent follows is in HQ (`workflows/map/guide.md`).

## How to use it

- Every plan starts at the top and works down: the vision, the year, the six-week game, the ideal week, the real week. Recovery first.
- When something in a lower layer pulls away from a higher one, your agent says so, once, plainly.
- Looking back (layer 6) is where the year, the game and the ideal week get adjusted. The vision only changes when you say so.
- Every question of every layer has its line in the layer's checklist: answered, parked (why, and when it comes back), later (when) or open. Your agent ticks it as you go, and holds a layer against the guide, line by line, before it's done.
